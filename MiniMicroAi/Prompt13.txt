Jab admin gram manual deposit karne ke liye form fill karta hai par "GRAM verification unavailable; please retry" ye error aa raha hai fix karo. Yesa karo manual deposit me me sirf tx hash/link dalun aur Blockchain se verify ho aur fir database me dekhe agar Blockchain se verify aur database me nahi hoga to database me deposit save ho jayega aur user ko message chala jayega. Aur ek manual name ka tab add karo jisme chain select karun aur tx hash dalun aur wo verify karke deposit kar de, aur ek deposit dobara na mile. Mujhe gram chain par sak ho raha hai ki wo kam kar raha hai ya nahi, isliye tum use verify karo aur deposit 2-3 latest deposits.
